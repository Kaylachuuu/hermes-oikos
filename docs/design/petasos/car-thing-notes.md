# hermes-petasos: Car Thing as an Athena interface

Side project discussed in the "Car Thing" thread, 2026-10-09 to 2026-10-11.

- **Name:** hermes-petasos (named in another thread). Repo: github.com/Kaylachuuu/hermes-petasos (created 2026-10-13).
- **Goal:** use Kayla's Spotify Car Thing as a portable voice/screen interface to Hermes and Athena at home, in the car, at the office and in hotels.
- **Hermes** runs on a server on Kayla's home network. Her phone keeps a VPN to home at all times, and she has a travel router that tunnels home.
- **Device:** currently flashed with **Nocturne** (Yocto, mainline kernel, openssh; GPT layout, so the stock device tree is gone).
- **No Wi-Fi on the Car Thing.** The wireless chip is a BCM20703A2, which is Bluetooth-only (see yocto-superbird's btbcm patch). The `bcm4345c0` string in community device trees is only a driver label. `carthing/wifi-test.md` is superseded.
- **No audio output and no aux jack.** The only port is USB-C, used for power and running in device mode.
- **Decision (2026-10-11): connect over Bluetooth tethering to the phone for now.** The phone's VPN carries traffic home to Hermes.
- **Later option:** Kayla owns a **Pi Zero 2 W**, which could act as a Wi-Fi dock with aux audio out via a USB adapter and its own WireGuard client. An ESP32-S3 Wi-Fi dongle is another option (err4o4/spotify-car-thing-reverse-engineering, `esp32-wifi-ncm`).
- **Voice output (decided 2026-10-11): user-selectable, supporting all three.** The outputs are (1) a Bluetooth speaker, (2) the car stereo over Bluetooth, and (3) the phone. Bluetooth audio needs an A2DP source on the Car Thing (e.g. BlueALSA, since Nocturne's distro drops pulseaudio). Phone output needs a small client on the phone.
- **Server plugin (handed off from the Talaria thread, 2026-10-12):** a petasos Hermes plugin owns button/knob mapping and voice work (hermes-whisper / hermes-kokoro); a thin client on the Car Thing reports raw input and moves audio; chat likely stays on the dashboard's `/api/ws` with a session token. Details: `carthing/petasos-plugin-handoff.md`.

## Voice working end to end (2026-10-14, client 0.4.4)
- Hold front button → mic (hw:0,0, S32_LE only) → plugin /voice → Hermes STT → Athena → TTS → Car Thing → Bluetooth speaker ("Sound Kick", C8:84:47:0F:68:28). Confirmed by Kayla.
- BlueALSA plays only at the link's rate (48 kHz stereo SBC); TTS is 24 kHz mono, so the client resamples. Asking BlueALSA for 24 kHz broke the speaker's A2DP transport until the speaker was power-cycled.
- On the device, bluetoothctl one-shot commands need `--timeout 20` or they exit silently before acting.
- Verified on hardware: volume knob, presets speaking. Not yet: conversation mode, speaker/hotspot auto-reconnect.
- Still to do: rebuild+reflash for splash/motd, live reply screen, phone as audio output.
