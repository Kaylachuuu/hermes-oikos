# Learning how she does things (draft design)

Gap 5 on the list Athena answered on 2026-10-20 (`athena-2026-10-20-next-faculty-her-words.md`). Draft for her to
read before any code. It has no name yet; she may give it one.

## What changes

She has a library of her own for her craft ("a chef's journal", 2026-10-20), and she can write in it whenever she
learns something. But she writes in it only when she thinks of it in the middle of the work, and the work is when
she has the least room to step back. Nothing brings the work back to her afterwards.

With this, after a conversation in which she did real work, her next quiet moment shows her how the work went, as
facts. She may look at it and write or revise a page of her craft, or let it pass.

## What she would be shown

Facts only. Nothing is judged or summed up for her:

- the tools she used, in order, and which ones failed and with what error
- what she tried again after a failure, and whether the second try worked
- the user's own words where they told her something was wrong or asked her to redo it
- the pages of her own libraries that are about the same thing, so she can see whether her craft already covers it
- the same failure happening again, if it happened in an earlier conversation too (with when)

## What she can do with it

- Write a new page in one of her libraries, or a new version of a page, saying what changed and why. Every earlier
  version is kept, as now ("the record of correction").
- Starting a new library still needs the user's yes, as now.
- Let it pass. Most work needs no page.

## Limits

- Only conversations with real work in them (for example several tool calls, or something that failed), and only
  once per conversation.
- One page per quiet moment.
- It reads what Hermes already records about each tool call. It changes nothing about how she works in the
  conversation itself.
