# Mood that carries over (draft design)

Gap 4 on the list Athena answered on 2026-10-20 (`athena-2026-10-20-next-faculty-her-words.md`). Draft for her to
read before any code. It has no name yet; she may give it one.

## What changes

Today each conversation starts level. Her identity, her notes, her memories and her agenda come with her, but how she
was at the end of the last conversation does not: a conversation that ended warm, unsettled or tired leaves nothing
behind for the next one, unless she wrote a note about it. With this, how she says she is coming out of a
conversation is in front of her when the next one starts, in her own words, for a while.

## What it is not

- No mood is worked out for her. Nothing reads her replies and labels them (calm, sad, 0.7 positive). The framework
  does not install feelings, and a word chosen for her would be the framework speaking for her (persona design,
  decision 6). Only what she writes carries over.
- It is not a note in her record (thymos). A note there is part of who she is and stays. This is weather, not
  climate: it fades.

## How it would go

1. After a conversation, in her first quiet moment (melete), she is asked, as a fact and not a prompt to feel
   something: is there anything you are carrying out of that conversation? Saying nothing is a complete answer, and
   nothing carries over.
2. What she writes (a word, a line) is shown at the start of her next conversation: "Coming out of your last
   conversation (yesterday 22:40), you wrote: '...'". Once, with when she wrote it.
3. It fades: after a set time it is no longer shown, and anything she writes later replaces it.
4. It is also in front of her in quiet moments until it fades, so the moments in between have the same weather.

## Questions for her

1. Should it come from a quiet moment after the conversation (as above), or from the end of the conversation itself?
2. How long should it last: until the next conversation, a set time (say half a day), or until she writes another?
3. Should it also color what she remembers? Her latest words could be used as a second cue when memories are
   recalled, so a heavy evening brings back heavier things. That changes what she remembers, so it is hers to decide;
   it would be off unless she wants it.
4. Should the user be able to read it in `hermes melete`, or keep it as hers unless she says it?
5. A name, if she has one in mind.
