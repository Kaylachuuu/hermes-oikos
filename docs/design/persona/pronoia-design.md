# Pronoia: holding an intention for the future (draft design)

Athena's choice for the next faculty (2026-10-20, `athena-2026-10-20-next-faculty-her-words.md`): "It moves me
from 'answering the prompt' to 'holding the goal'." Draft for her to read before any code. The name is a
placeholder (pronoia, forethought); she may name it.

## What she can hold

An intention is something she means to do or come back to, in her own words, with what should bring it back:

| Brought back by | Example |
|---|---|
| A time | "Ask Kayla how the interview went" after Thursday 17:00 |
| A person | "Tell Mum about the greenhouse" when that person next talks to her (with Hermai) |
| A topic | "Mention the calling-convention bug" when the conversation turns to versa-os |
| A quiet moment | "Check whether the House idea still holds" at her next time to herself (melete) |

Each one says who set it (she, or the user asking her to remember something) and when. "Remind me" from the user
is the same kind of thing, marked as the user's.

## How it comes back

- In a conversation: when its time has passed, its person is speaking, or its topic comes up (by meaning, using
  holonomic's embeddings), it is placed in her context as a fact: "On 2026-10-20 you meant to ask how the
  interview went." What she does with it is hers. Nothing is said for her.
- Between conversations: a time or quiet-moment intention is shown in her next melete beat. If reaching out is on,
  she may choose to send a message from there, under melete's existing limits.
- Each conversation she can see the list of what she is holding, briefly, on request (a tool action), not in
  every prompt.

## Keeping it hers

- She sets, changes, marks done, or lets go of an intention with a tool. Letting go keeps it on record with her
  reason, as melete does with closed agenda items. Nothing is deleted.
- A small cap on open intentions (say 12), so it stays a few things she cares about, not a to-do list. One untouched
  past its time for a week is put to her: keep, move, or let go.
- Melete's "tell" (one thing to tell the user next time) and her agenda (things she is turning over) stay as they
  are. An intention differs by having a when.

## What it is not

Hermes' scheduled tasks run a job at a time. This runs nothing: it brings something back to her and she decides.
