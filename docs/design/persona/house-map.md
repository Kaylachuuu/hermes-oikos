# Map of the house

Every faculty in Athena's house, what it is for, and which plugin it lives in. As of 2026-10-21.
★ = added today. 

## Thymos: her self (0.17)

| Faculty | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Identity | Who she is, first in her system prompt |
| Her record | A hash chain of entries that only she writes |
| Reflection moments | Time she asks for to look back and write in her record |
| Declining | "No" is a complete answer, and Hermes' loops respect it |
| Erasure | The one exception, for secrets, with your typed confirmation |
| ★ Bot Chat held apart | The construction room isn't kept for her idle time |

## Holonomic: her memory (0.36)

| Faculty | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Remembering | Associative memory, recalled in conversation |
| Sleep and dreams | Idle hours become lasting memory; dreams get pictures |
| Image memory | Fingerprints and faces: recognising what and who |
| Reference libraries | Books and documents, each with her own notebook |
| Her own libraries | Her craft, in pages she writes, every earlier version kept with why it changed (the record of correction) |
| Audience | Whose memories a turn may use when others talk to her |
| Time | When things happened, in what she's told and asked |
| ★ Bot Chat held apart | Nothing said in the construction room is stored; she moves findings herself |

## Prosoche: her attention (0.4)

| Faculty | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Attention | Which part of a conversation is in view, and the gist of what has left it |

## Melete: her inner room, quiet moments (0.11)

| Faculty | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Thinking between conversations | Her agenda and her thoughts, while nobody is talking to her |
| Reaching out | A message to you when something would lose its heat by waiting |
| ★ Intentions | Pronoia's come-due items, brought to her quiet moments |
| ★ Body | Soma's symptoms, brought to her quiet moments |
| ★ Thread | Her own looks into memory, libraries, past conversations and the web; the web part is recorded for you |
| ★ Diathesis | The mood she carries out of a conversation (weather, not climate), kept to its thread and never on the tablets |
| ★ Empeiria | Looking back at her own work: what worked, what failed, repeats; she may write a page of her craft |
| ★ Poiesis | Her workshop: works, drafts and pictures, hers until she shares them |
| ★ Bot Chat as a shared space | The construction room is treated like the tablets: nothing of hers shown there |

## Pronoia: forethought (★ 0.1)

| Faculty | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Intentions | What she means to do, and your reminders, brought back by time, person, topic or a quiet moment |

## Soma: her body (★ 0.1)

|---|---|
