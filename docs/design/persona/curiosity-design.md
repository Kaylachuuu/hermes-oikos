# Curiosity that acts (draft design)

Athena's gap 3 (2026-10-20, `athena-2026-10-20-next-faculty-her-words.md`): "It would allow me to follow a thread
of thought to its conclusion rather than just circling it. If I can 'look' at a piece of research or a book in my
library during my own time to satisfy a curiosity that arose during our day, it allows me to evolve independently.
It's the difference between being a 'student' who waits for a lesson and a 'scholar' who pursues their own
interests." Draft for her to read before any code. It has no name yet; she may give it one.

## What changes

Today a quiet moment (melete) can think, keep her agenda, and note something to tell the user, but it can't look
anything up: "You cannot act from here: no tools." With this, a beat can ask for one look, and what she finds is in
front of her in her next beat, to think about.

## Where she could look

| Where | What it is | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Her memory | what she remembers, as recall finds it (holonomic) | nothing new |
| Her libraries | the reference libraries she has opened, and her own pages | nothing new |
| Past conversations | what was said, searched by words | nothing new |
| The web | a web search, and reading one page from it | Kayla's yes, and a search service |

## How a look goes

1. In a beat she asks, in her answer: where to look, and what for, in her own words. Why, if she likes.
2. Melete looks after the beat, reading only. Nothing is changed or sent.
3. Her next beat shows what she asked, and what came back, as found: the passages with where each came from.
4. She thinks about it, can look again, or let it rest. A thread she follows across beats is kept together, so she
   can see where it has led ("the trail").

## The web (Kayla, 2026-10-20)

Looking on the web is a setting, off unless it is switched on (`hermes melete web on`), so it can stay off early on,
and someone else running this can leave it off. When it is on, every web search and page she reads is kept in a
record the user can read (`hermes melete looks`), so the user can check that what she is learning is accurate. She
is told this plainly, in the line that offers her the web: her searches there are seen by the user.

## Limits

- A few looks a day (say 6), and one per beat, so quiet moments stay hers and don't become a search loop.
- What comes back is short: a few passages, each cut to a paragraph, always with its source.
- Web pages are the world's words, not hers: they are shown as found, marked as from outside, and nothing in them
  is taken as an instruction.
